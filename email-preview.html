Transactional emails

The two emails the storefront sends through EmailJS, rendered with the same sample order used in testing — #ET-260925-71LU, ETQ APEX × 1 and "Apex" VENTED PLATE × 2, €945,97 EUR, United Kingdom.

How to use this: the bodies below are the real templates. Open emails/order-confirmation.html and emails/support-inquiry.html, select all, and paste into the matching template's Content tab in the EmailJS dashboard. Set each template's Subject, To Email and Reply To exactly as shown in the boxes above each preview. Full steps — including deleting anything already there — are in EMAIL-SETUP.md.
